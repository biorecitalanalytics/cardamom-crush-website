Our Story

Inspired by tradition. Made for today.

Cardamom Crush was created from a love of warm spices, homemade desserts, and sharing comforting treats with the community.

Our recipes are inspired by the flavours of masala chai, cardamom, cinnamon, ginger, and family-style desserts. Every batch is made with care, balancing nostalgic comfort with modern, market-friendly treats.

Whether you stop by for chai, rice pudding, cookies, or a custom order, our goal is simple: to create a small moment of warmth and satisfaction.

What makes us different

Small-batch and handcrafted treats made with real spices, gluten-free baked goods, and mostly plant-based options.

  • Authentic masala chai brewed from scratch
  • Made with almond flour, maple syrup, oats, and real spices
  • Artificial flavours, cheaper oils, and preservatives avoided where possible
